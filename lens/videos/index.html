浮城龍舟

Floating City: Dragon Boat

香港是最早舉辦國際龍舟賽的城市。影片以龍舟為切入點，講述燈塔守 花維路畢生協助香港水上人融入陸上社會、並以龍舟競渡幫助他們重拾 信心的故事。透過學者、家屬與漁民訪談，作品呈現香港傳統與現代、 東方與西方之間的交會。

Dragon boating provides an entry point into the story of a Hong Kong lighthouse keeper who devoted his life to helping the city’s boat-dwelling communities adapt to life ashore. Through interviews with scholars, family members, and fishers, the documentary explores how a shared sporting tradition helped restore confidence while bringing Hong Kong’s traditional and modern, Eastern and Western worlds into conversation.

Documentary Nagasaki

灯塔照丝路

西学东渐之日本长崎

影片聚焦長崎的常燈鼻、伊王島與大瀨崎三座燈塔，追溯日本燈塔從 傳統石塔到西式設計的轉變。它們既記錄海上絲綢之路上的文化與技術 交流，也見證日本從傳統社會走向現代化的歷程。

Focusing on three lighthouses in Nagasaki, the film traces Japan’s shift from traditional stone towers to Western-style lighthouse design. These coastal structures record centuries of maritime exchange and illuminate Japan’s broader transition from a traditional society to a modern state.

Documentary Intangible Cultural Heritage

麻雀獻藝

The Craft of Hand-Carved Mahjong

麻將的玩法蘊含市井文化與兵家思想，牌面亦流露獨特的中國美學。 影片走進香港非物質文化遺產手雕麻雀技藝傳承人的家庭，傾聽一門 傳統手藝如何在代際之間延續並獲得新的生命。

Mahjong carries street culture, strategic thinking, and a distinctly Chinese visual language within its tiles and rules. This documentary enters the home of a family preserving Hong Kong’s intangible cultural heritage of hand-carved mahjong tiles, tracing how a traditional craft is carried forward and renewed across generations.
